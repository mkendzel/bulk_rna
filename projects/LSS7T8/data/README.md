## Repo for experiments LSS7T8 data

Data generated for this experiments comes from 9 mice, 2 treatments (Mock, CL+DOPC or CL alone), and two tissues (liver and spleen).

Originally this experiment was thought to be an error as the LNP preparations were thought to be messed up. Analyzed after R2SDHF (which was performed as after this experiment was thought to be a waster). Has the exact same format as R2SDHF. Analysis was accepted after the results of R2SDHF did not meet expectations.

**Could potentially combine this with R2SDHF to increase the samples and just control for each as a batch effect.**
